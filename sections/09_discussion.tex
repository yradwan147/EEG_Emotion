\section{Discussion: Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.} Saturation is empirical and
\emph{scoped to FACED-9 with a measurable V-axis substrate}: the
boxed statement describes rather than proves, the $[0.62, 0.66]$
transition is dataset-specific, SEED-V is too small to localise its
own cliff, and we make no claim that the regularity holds in other
tasks, modalities, or substrates. Probe transfers (SST-2, cohort
EEG, classifier convergence) are direction-existence claims, not
neuroscience claims; the posterior topography is a video-paradigm
statement, not a refutation of FAA~\citep{davidson1992anterior}. KD
prototypes are interchangeable with random orthonormal directions,
so the SOTA gain is structural, not semantic; probe and SOTA
reconcile via saturation. Per-subject residual adaptation
(Appendix~\ref{app:discussion-extras}) is the direct test.
