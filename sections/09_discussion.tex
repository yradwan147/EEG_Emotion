\section{Discussion: Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations.} Saturation is empirical, not analytical: we
locate the FACED-9 transition at BACC $[0.62, 0.66]$, but the
threshold magnitude is dataset-specific, the boxed statement
describes rather than proves, and SEED-V is not large enough to
localise its own cliff. The probe transfers (SST-2, cohort EEG,
classifier convergence) are direction-existence claims, not
neuroscience claims; the cohort topography is a paradigm statement
for video-evoked emotion, not a refutation of frontal-alpha
asymmetry~\citep{davidson1992anterior}. The KD prototypes that
contribute $+0.0096$ to the SOTA recipe are interchangeable with
random orthonormal directions: the gain is structural, not semantic,
and the probe and the SOTA gain are reconciled only via saturation.

\paragraph{Future work.} Per-subject residual adaptation
(Appendix~\ref{app:discussion-extras}) is the most direct positive
test; locating the transition cliff on a non-emotion task would
test whether
bias--variance~\citep{geman1992neural,frankle2020linearmode}
quantitatively predicts where concept supervision stops helping.
The regularity is falsifiable in the obvious direction.
