\textbf{FACED 9-class SOTA recipe cascade: from EMOD to a $0.6948$
ensemble.}
Each bar is one recipe step; grey bars are prior published baselines
(CBraMod $0.572$~\citep{wang2025cbramod}, EmotionKD
$0.628$~\citep{liu2023emotionkd}, EMOD
$0.6287$~\citep{chen2025emod}), blue bars are this work, dark blue
is the locked SOTA. Numbers above bars are mean balanced accuracy;
error bars show $\pm$ standard deviation over $\geq 5$ seeds where
applicable. Green annotations show stepwise $\Delta\mathrm{BACC}$.
The $10$-checkpoint ensemble ($5\!\times\!e{=}100\,+\,5\!\times\!e{=}150$,
all $d{=}6$) reaches $0.6948$ BACC, $+0.066$ absolute ($+10.5\%$
relative) over EMOD's $0.6287$ (the previous FACED-9 SOTA) and
$+0.017$ over our own best val-selected single checkpoint ($0.6755$).
The SOTA does not require any V-axis loss in training, and the
LLM-derived class prototypes used by the KD step are interchangeable
with random orthonormal directions ($\Delta\le 0.003$, see
Table~\ref{tab:sota}).
\emph{Test split: subjects $100$--$122$ ($n{=}1932$ trials), identical
across all rows.}
