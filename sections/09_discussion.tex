\section{Discussion: Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.} Saturation is empirical:
the boxed statement describes rather than proves, the FACED-9
transition at $[0.62, 0.66]$ BACC is dataset-specific, and SEED-V is
too small to localise its own cliff. Probe transfers (SST-2, cohort
EEG, classifier convergence) are direction-existence claims, not
neuroscience claims; the posterior topography is a video-paradigm
statement, not a refutation of
FAA~\citep{davidson1992anterior}. KD prototypes are interchangeable
with random orthonormal directions, so the SOTA gain is structural,
not semantic; probe and SOTA reconcile via saturation. Per-subject
residual adaptation (Appendix~\ref{app:discussion-extras}) is the direct test.
