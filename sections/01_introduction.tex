\paragraph{Contributions.} \textbf{(1)} The saturation regularity:
$25$ interventions, $16/25$ statistically significant negatives,
monotonic destruction in $5$ families, transition at FACED-9 BACC
$[0.62, 0.66]$ (\S\ref{sec:saturation}). \textbf{(2)} Mechanism check:
V-axis supervision moves the saturated class-PC1 by
$\Delta|r| = +0.01$ to $+0.36$ but moves the within-class residual by
$\sim 10^{-7}$, a numerical zero (\S\ref{sec:saturation}).
\textbf{(3)} A residual-diversity ensemble that turns the mechanism
into a positive prescription: $r{=}{+}0.74$ between per-checkpoint
residual encoding and leave-one-out contribution, and a new FACED-9
state of the art at $\mathbf{0.6948}$
(\S\ref{sec:convergence},~\S\ref{sec:sota}). \textbf{(4)} A probe for
