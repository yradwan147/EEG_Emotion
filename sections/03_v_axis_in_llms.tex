\section{Probing the Saturated Concept: A Valence Direction in LLMs}
\label{sec:vaxis-llms}

To test that the saturation regularity operates on a real semantic
axis rather than a noise dimension, we extract one explicitly. The
construction uses nine sentences: given a language model with hidden
dimension $D$ and the FACED 9-emotion taxonomy, we author one short
emotion-evocative story per class, generate $n{=}50$ paraphrases per
story by independent LLM calls, and take the mean last-token hidden
state at $L_{\max}{-}1$ to obtain nine class centroids
$c_1,\dots,c_9 \in \mathbb{R}^D$. Mean-centred PCA gives the V-axis
as the unit vector along $\mathrm{PC}_1$, oriented so
$\langle c_{\mathrm{Joy}}, v\rangle > 0$. Qwen3-4B at $L{=}35/36$ is
our lead model (chosen for compute parsimony among the seven Qwen3
sizes whose behavioural-valence correlations sit within $0.01$ of
one another, $0.94$--$0.95$, Appendix~\ref{app:per-llm-deep-dive}).
The recipe is robust to paraphrase budget (zero-shot SST-2 AUC $0.74$
at $n{=}1$, rising to $0.832$ at $n{=}50$) and to prompt rephrasing
(mean cross-prompt $r{>}0.93$). Full protocol and quality controls
are in Appendix~\ref{app:vaxis-protocol}.

\paragraph{The same direction across $14$ LLMs.}
We extract the V-axis from $14$ language models spanning three orders
of magnitude---all six dense Qwen3 sizes from 0.6B to 32B,
Llama-4-Scout-17B, Mistral-7B, Phi-2-2.7B, Pythia-1.4B, BLOOM-560M,
TinyLlama-1.1B, Gemma-27B, and Gemma-4-31B. Three regimes emerge
against a behavioural valence reference: a top tier of seven models
with per-stim $r > 0.85$ (all six Qwen3 sizes plus Mistral-7B), a
middle tier of four ($0.6 \le r \le 0.85$), and an out-tier of three
with $r{<}0.4$ (Pythia-1.4B, TinyLlama-1.1B, BLOOM-560M). The
pairwise off-diagonal decomposes cleanly: \emph{within} Qwen3 the
V-axes are essentially identical ($r{=}{+}0.995 \pm 0.003$ across
$15$ pairs), \emph{across} families the correlation drops to
$+0.585 \pm 0.289$ over $48$ pairs. Convergence is tight within a
modern LLM family and looser across families.

\paragraph{External validity.}
A single Qwen3-4B run recovers SST-2 sentiment at zero-shot AUC
$\mathbf{0.832}$~\citep{socher2013recursive}, matching a supervised
logistic-regression baseline trained on $5$k in-domain examples
($0.837$), beating a SBERT prototype-cosine zero-shot baseline
($0.793$;~\citealp{reimers2019sbert}), and within $0.08$ of a $355$M-param
RoBERTa-large-MNLI zero-shot entailment classifier
($0.912$;~\citealp{liu2019roberta}). The same direction reaches
$0.938$ on Yelp Polarity~\citep{yelp2015}; the full sentiment
table (IMDB, TweetEval, Rotten Tomatoes) and word-level lexicon
recoveries (Hu \& Liu, Warriner) are in
Appendix~\ref{app:per-llm-deep-dive}. An Arditi-style same-model
ablation~\citep{arditi2024refusal}---projecting the V-axis out of
Qwen3-4B's own penultimate features---collapses the V-axis zero-shot
decoder on SST-2 from $0.832$ to chance ($0.505$). A linear-probe
classifier retrained on the now-ablated features only loses $0.0019$
AUC (still $z{=}{+}9.8$ above a $20$-direction matched-norm null), so
the V-axis is the direction Qwen3-4B uses for zero-shot sentiment,
even though some discriminative content remains in the orthogonal
complement.

\paragraph{A late-layer phenomenon.}
The cosine between the input-layer and penultimate-layer V-axis stays
below $0.30$ across Qwen3 sizes; the same V-shape---axes that
converge only in the last few layers---appears across five LM
families and across BERT, RoBERTa, and DeBERTa, peaking at
$L_{\max}{-}1$ and ruling out tokeniser or training-objective
artefacts. Within this V-shape pattern, Gemma-4 is the lone outlier,
with a text-side V-axis that peaks at $L{=}1$ but brain alignment
that survives at mid-layers (\S\ref{sec:vaxis-brain}). The recipe is
also concept-generic, cross-lingual, and cross-modal: $20/20$ further
concepts produce a working axis ($17$ exceed AUC $0.95$,
Appendix~\ref{app:concept-library}); on
mT0-base~\citep{muennighoff2022mt0} the recipe exceeds the English
ceiling on Japanese, Arabic, and Russian; a CLIP-text V-axis ranks
OASIS~\citep{kurdi2017oasis,radford2021clip} valence at $r{=}0.869$
and arousal at $r{=}0.803$.
