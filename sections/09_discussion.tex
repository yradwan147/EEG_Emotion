\section{Discussion: Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

Saturation reframes any auxiliary-loss result: a reported gain is
uninterpretable without the receiving baseline's saturation state. The
robust, cross-domain half is the \emph{absence of gain} at convergence
(aligned $\approx$ random, $0/20$ non-EEG branches; on CIFAR-10 the
benefit merely vanishes); significant \emph{harm} is established on the
converged FACED-9 backbone. The probe transfers are direction-existence, not mechanism,
claims, and the cohort signal is population-level
(Appendix~\ref{app:topography-deep-dive}). Three limitations bound the
broader reading. \textbf{(i)}~We do not claim architecture-independent
convergence: the pooled encoding--accuracy correlation is
pseudo-replicated, scoping convergence to seeds of one architecture. \textbf{(ii)}~The cross-model LLM
valence geometry is largely input-driven (an untrained model reproduces
it): a probe, not a learned representation.
\textbf{(iii)}~Transfer is content-specific (valence across text, vision,
and EEG; arousal only in vision); whether the decomposition generalises
to other concepts is open.
