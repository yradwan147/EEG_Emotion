\textbf{Forest plot of all $25$ V-axis-as-supervision interventions,
ranked by $\Delta\mathrm{BACC}$.}
Each row is one intervention; bar length is the change in FACED
9-class balanced accuracy versus the matched-recipe vanilla baseline
(paired-mean over $\geq 3$ seeds where available, single-seed for
catastrophic failures). Significance markers: $\ast{\ast}\ast$
$p{<}0.001$, $\ast\ast$ $p{<}0.01$, $\ast$ $p{<}0.05$, ns otherwise
(paired $t$-test, $5$ seeds). Colour tier: dark red = catastrophic
destruction ($\Delta{<}{-}0.10$), red = significant negative,
orange = non-significant negative, light blue = non-significant
positive. \emph{No intervention produces a statistically significant
positive effect at the strong baseline}: every loss either lands
within seed noise of zero or actively hurts. This is the empirical
content of the saturation regularity~(\S\ref{sec:saturation}).
