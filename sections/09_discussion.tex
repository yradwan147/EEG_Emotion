\section{Discussion: Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

Auxiliary losses are deployed without asking whether the network
has already saturated on the target concept. We make three steps:
a saturation regularity that organises $25$ recipe failures, a
residual-diversity ensemble that turns the mechanism into FACED-9
SOTA, and a nine-sentence V-axis probe demonstrating the saturated
concept is a real semantic axis shared with language. The
regularity is scoped to FACED-9 with a V-axis substrate (no claim
beyond), probe transfers are direction-existence rather than
neuroscience claims, and the posterior topography is a
video-paradigm statement, not a refutation of FAA~\citep{davidson1992anterior}.
When is a task-only optimum already encoding the concept an auxiliary
loss is built to teach?---per-subject residual adaptation
(Appendix~\ref{app:discussion-extras}) is the immediate test.
