\section{Discussion: Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.} Saturation is empirical, not
analytical: the boxed statement describes rather than proves, the
FACED-9 transition at $[0.62, 0.66]$ BACC is dataset-specific in
magnitude, and SEED-V is not large enough to localise its own cliff.
The probe transfers (SST-2, cohort EEG, classifier convergence) are
direction-existence claims, not neuroscience claims; the posterior
topography is a paradigm statement for video-evoked emotion, not a
refutation of frontal-alpha asymmetry~\citep{davidson1992anterior}.
The KD prototypes are interchangeable with random orthonormal
directions: the SOTA gain is structural, not semantic, and the probe
and SOTA findings are reconciled only via saturation. The most direct
positive test for the regularity is per-subject residual adaptation
(Appendix~\ref{app:discussion-extras}); locating the cliff on a
non-emotion task is the obvious generalisation.
