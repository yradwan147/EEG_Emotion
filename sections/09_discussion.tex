\section{Discussion: Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.} Saturation is empirical, not
analytical: the boxed statement describes rather than proves; the
FACED-9 transition at $[0.62, 0.66]$ BACC is dataset-specific; SEED-V
is too small to localise its own cliff. The probe transfers (SST-2,
cohort EEG, classifier convergence) are direction-existence claims,
not neuroscience claims; the posterior topography is a video-paradigm
statement, not a refutation of
FAA~\citep{davidson1992anterior}. KD prototypes are interchangeable with random orthonormal directions:
the SOTA gain is structural, not semantic. Probe and SOTA reconcile
only via saturation. Per-subject residual adaptation (Appendix~\ref{app:discussion-extras}) is the direct test.
