\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Concept-direction supervision is the standard recipe for adding
semantic structure to a neural classifier. A target direction $v$ is
specified in feature space---a class prototype, an LLM-derived
embedding, a known affective axis---and a loss is added that pulls
the network's penultimate features toward $v$. The variants span
seven years of representation-alignment literature: auxiliary
regression toward fixed targets, knowledge
distillation~\citep{hinton2015distilling}, representational similarity
analysis~\citep{kriegeskorte2008rsa}, contrastive
losses~\citep{khosla2020supcon,vandenoord2018cpc}, parameter-efficient
adapters~\citep{hu2022lora,liu2022ia3}, and channel-targeted
topographic supervision. The shared assumption is that the loss
provides information the task does not.

We test $25$ such interventions on FACED 9-class EEG emotion
classification~\citep{chen2023faced}. None help. Sixteen produce
statistically significant accuracy decrements
($\Delta\mathrm{BACC} \le -0.013$, $p<0.05$, paired across five seeds);
zero produce gains. Five intervention families exhibit
\emph{monotonic destruction} as the loss weight is raised, with no
positive setpoint to find. The transition from neutral to harmful is
sharp and sits at FACED-9 balanced accuracy in $[0.62, 0.66]$: the
same intervention that lies within seed noise of zero on a weak
baseline produces a significant negative on the strong recipe.

We call this regularity \emph{saturation}. It is not overfitting:
overfitting is memorisation of the training set, while saturation is
a property of how task supervision and auxiliary supervision interact
once both target the same direction. Once the class-mean features
have absorbed the concept from task labels alone, $v$ is no longer
free real estate for supervision. The auxiliary loss perturbs the
saturated component while leaving the orthogonal within-class
residual---the subspace where seeds differ and ensembling recovers
signal---untouched.

The same mechanism gives a positive prescription: if the residual is
what carries information past the saturated basin, ensembling across
the residual should recover the gain that supervising it cannot.
Following this prescription on FACED-9 yields a new state of the art
at $\mathbf{0.6948}$ balanced accuracy on a 10-checkpoint softmax
ensemble (best val-selected single $\mathbf{0.6755}$), $+21.5\%$ over
CBraMod's $0.572$~\citep{wang2025cbramod} and $+10.6\%$ over
EmotionKD's $0.628$~\citep{liu2023emotionkd}. The class prototypes
used by knowledge distillation in our recipe are interchangeable with
random orthonormal directions ($\Delta\mathrm{BACC} \le 0.003$): the
gain is structural, not semantic.

A probe is needed for the saturation claim---what is the concept the
network already encodes? We use a one-dimensional \emph{valence
direction} ($v$), extracted by mean-centred PCA from the late-layer
activations of nine short LLM-generated emotion stories, one per
FACED class~\citep{russell1980circumplex,warriner2013norms}. The
same direction recovers SST-2 sentiment zero-shot at AUC $0.832$ from
a single Qwen3-4B run~\citep{socher2013recursive}, predicts
cohort-averaged EEG of $123$ subjects watching emotional videos at
$r{=}0.87$ ($p<10^{-9}$), and emerges spontaneously in $36$ EEG
classifiers trained only on the 9-class label, with class-PC1
alignment $r{=}{+}0.885$ and within-class residual alignment
$r{=}{+}0.738$ across checkpoints. All five claims (probe existence,
brain alignment, classifier convergence, saturation, residual
ensembling) replicate on SEED-V~\citep{liu2022seedv}.

\paragraph{Contributions.} \textbf{(1)} The saturation regularity:
$25$ interventions, $16/25$ statistically significant negatives,
monotonic destruction in $5$ families, transition at FACED-9 BACC
$[0.62, 0.66]$ (\S\ref{sec:saturation}). \textbf{(2)} Mechanism check:
V-axis supervision moves the saturated class-PC1 by
$\Delta|r| = +0.01$ to $+0.36$ but moves the within-class residual by
$\sim 10^{-7}$, a numerical zero (\S\ref{sec:saturation}).
\textbf{(3)} A residual-diversity ensemble that turns the mechanism
into a positive prescription: $r{=}{+}0.74$ between per-checkpoint
residual encoding and leave-one-out contribution, and a new FACED-9
state of the art at $\mathbf{0.6948}$
(\S\ref{sec:convergence},~\S\ref{sec:sota}). \textbf{(4)} A probe for
the saturated concept: a one-dimensional valence direction extracted
from $9$ LLM stories that simultaneously recovers SST-2 sentiment,
predicts cohort EEG, and emerges in EEG classifiers
(\S\ref{sec:vaxis-llms}--\S\ref{sec:topography}). \textbf{(5)}
Cross-dataset replication on SEED-V, including saturation, residual
ablation, and the SOTA prescription
(\S\ref{sec:vaxis-brain-seedv}).
